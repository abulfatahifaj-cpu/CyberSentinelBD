02 / SECURITY CHECKLIST

Make safety a habit.

Simple steps that make your online life safer.

⌁
01

Strong passwords

Use a unique, long password for every important account. A password manager can help you keep them organized.

ACCOUNT SECURITY
✳
02

Turn on 2-step verification

Add another layer of protection to your email, messaging apps, and social accounts wherever it is available.

EXTRA PROTECTION
↗
03

Spot suspicious links

Pause before opening unexpected links or files. Check the sender and website address carefully.

PHISHING AWARENESS
◈
04

Keep devices updated

Install official updates for your phone, computer, browser, and apps to receive important security fixes.

DEVICE SAFETY
◎
05

Protect your privacy

Review app permissions and privacy settings. Share personal information only when it is necessary and appropriate.

DIGITAL PRIVACY
⊙
06

Never share OTPs

Never share verification codes, passwords, or recovery codes with callers or messages—even if they claim to be support staff.
